Frequently Asked Questions

How is "Safe to Spend" calculated? +
We take your monthly salary + extra income - expenses, then divide by days left until payday. This gives your daily budget.
Does this work offline? +
Yes. After first load, everything works without internet. Your data is stored locally on your device only.
Is my financial data secure? +
100%. All data stays on your device in localStorage. We never send it to any server. Export backups to keep it safe.
How do notifications work? +
Click "Enable Alerts" to get reminders 3 days before payday and daily budget summaries. You can disable anytime in browser settings.
Are investment suggestions financial advice? +
No. These are general educational ideas based on your savings amount. Always consult a licensed financial advisor before investing.
