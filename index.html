Frequently Asked Questions

How is "Safe to Spend" calculated? +
Your salary + income - expenses, divided by days left until payday. Keeps you from going broke.
Does this work offline? +
Yes. Load once with internet, then works forever offline. Your data never leaves your phone.
Why did I get a white screen before? +
Fixed now. The app crashed when data was empty. Now it resets to welcome screen instead.
How do I clear all data? +
Go to Settings → Clear All Data. This resets the app to welcome screen.
Are investment suggestions financial advice? +
No. These are general educational ideas. Always consult a licensed financial advisor before investing.
