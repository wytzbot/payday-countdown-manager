Privacy Policy for Payday Pro

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data Storage

Payday Pro stores all your financial data locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not collect, transmit, store on servers, or have access to your salary, transactions, or personal financial information.

2. Data You Provide

You voluntarily enter: monthly salary, payday date, expenses, and income notes. This data never leaves your device unless you manually export a backup file.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and collect data per their privacy policy. You can opt out at https://adssettings.google.com. We do not share your financial data with Google or any advertiser.

4. Permissions

Payday Pro may request notification permission to send budget alerts. This is optional and can be denied.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Educational Content

All savings examples shown in the app are for educational purposes only and do not constitute financial advice.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use means you accept changes.

Questions? Email: support@paydaypro.app
